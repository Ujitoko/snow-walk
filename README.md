# Finger walking on snow

A browser demo of pseudo-haptic heaviness on a touchscreen.
Swipe the snow toward you on each side and compare: on the left (shallow snow) the ground follows your finger exactly;
on the right (deep snow) it follows only 22% of your finger's motion, which makes walking feel heavier.

Open it on a phone: https://ujitoko.github.io/snow-walk/

Everything is in `index.html` (no build step, no server). Footstep sounds are cut from the Yubi-Toko demo video.
